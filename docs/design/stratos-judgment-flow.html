02 — The logic

Gesture maps to stakes, not to effort

TikTok makes swipe feel "easiest" because there each item is self-contained, a wrong swipe is free, and the swipe only means next. In a judgment tool, none of that is automatic — so bind each gesture to what a mistake with it would cost.

GestureCost of a mistakeIts job in StratOS
Swipe
lateral
 ~zero · instantly reversible Move between self-contained cases and tensions — the cheap, exploratory browse. Nothing is decided by arriving somewhere.
Scroll
vertical
 none · it's just depth Drill into the evidence behind one verdict — OBSERVED → ESTIMATED → FOG → assumptions. Reading, never committing.
Tap
deliberate
 you meant it · locks state The single act that commits — place a pole, accept the verdict, stake exposure. The bet-slip moment. Guard it; make it feel weighty.
